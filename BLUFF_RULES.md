# 🃏 BLUFF MASTER (CHEAT / I DOUBT IT / 420) — OFFICIAL RULES

Welcome to **Bluff Master**, the legendary traditional card game of deception, psychology, poker faces, and strategic timing. The rules below govern online multiplayer matches on this platform.

---

## 🎯 1. Game Objective
The primary goal in Bluff Master is simple:
**Be the first player to successfully get rid of all 52 cards in your hand!**

---

## 👥 2. Players & Card Distribution
- **Deck**: Standard 52-card deck (Aces, 2 through 10, Jacks, Queens, Kings across all 4 suits: Hearts ♥, Diamonds ♦, Clubs ♣, Spades ♠).
- **Supported Players**: 2 to 5 players per room.
- **Dealing**: All 52 cards are thoroughly shuffled and dealt as evenly as possible to all seated players:
  - **2 Players**: 26 cards each.
  - **3 Players**: 17, 17, and 18 cards.
  - **4 Players**: 13 cards each.
  - **5 Players**: 10, 10, 10, 11, and 11 cards.
- **Hand View**: Each player sees all their own cards displayed in an interactive **Fan Hand**, automatically grouped and sorted by rank (A, 2, 3, ... K) so you can easily spot your duplicates and plan your bluffs.

---

## 🔄 3. How a Round Works (The Bluff Cycle)

### Step A: Leading a New Cycle
- At the start of the match (or whenever the table pile is cleared/swept), the active player is the **Cycle Leader**.
- The Cycle Leader selects **1 or more cards** (single, pair, triple, or four of a kind) to place **face down** onto the center table pile.
- The player declares a rank for their play:
  - *Example*: You select 3 cards and announce **"3 Aces"** (even if in reality you played 2 Aces and 1 Jack, or 3 Kings!).
  - You can tell the absolute **truth**, or play a complete **bluff**.
- The cards are placed face down in the center pile.

### Step B: The Next Player's Turn
Once a claim is active on the table, the turn moves clockwise to the next player. That player has **3 distinct options**:

#### Option 1: 🔍 SHOW (Call Bluff / Challenge)
- If you suspect that the previous player was lying about the cards they just played, click **"Show"**.
- The cards from that **latest play only** are immediately flipped face up for everyone at the table to see:
  - **BLUFF CAUGHT (Liar!):** If **even a single card** does not match the declared rank (e.g. they claimed "3 Aces" but one card is a Jack), the bluff is exposed!
    - The **accused player (the bluffer)** must pick up the **ENTIRE center discard pile** into their hand.
    - The challenger (who successfully caught them) wins the exchange and starts the next fresh cycle!
  - **HONEST PLAY (Truth!):** If **all cards** match the declared rank:
    - The **challenger** (who made a false accusation) must pick up the **ENTIRE center discard pile** into their hand.
    - The player who told the truth wins the exchange and starts the next fresh cycle!

#### Option 2: ➕ ADD CARDS (Continue the Claim)
- If you believe the previous player (or do not want to risk a challenge), you can contribute **1 or more cards** face down to the current cycle.
- These added cards are claimed to be the **same rank** currently active in the cycle:
  - *Example*: If the active claim is **Aces**, any cards you add are also claimed to be Aces.
  - Just like the leader, you may play actual Aces or bluff with any other cards!
- The total cards in the center pile increase, and the turn passes to the next player.

#### Option 3: ⏭️ PASS
- If you do not wish to play any cards or call Show, click **"Pass"**.
- Your turn is skipped and advances to the next player.

---

## 🧹 4. Clearing the Pile (Cycle Discard)
What happens if everyone passes?
- If a player plays cards, and **every other player in succession passes** without calling Show:
  - The turn travels full circle back to the player who made that last play.
  - Since nobody dared to challenge, that player has successfully defended their play!
  - The entire center pile is **CLEARED / SWEPT from the game** (discarded into the void — nobody has to pick it up!).
  - That player now gets to lead a **brand new cycle** and can declare any rank they choose.

---

## 🏆 5. Winning the Match & The Final Card Challenge
- When a player plays their final card(s) and their hand count reaches 0:
  - **They do not automatically win immediately.**
  - The next player (or remaining players) still gets the opportunity to call **"Show"** on that final play!
  - If someone calls Show and catches them bluffing on their last play, the player must pick up the entire pile and is right back in the game!
  - If the final play goes unchallenged (or is challenged and proven honest), that player officially **wins 1st Place!**
- The game continues among the remaining players to decide 2nd place, 3rd place, etc., until only 1 player remains with cards (the ultimate Bhabho/Loser).

---

## 💡 6. Expert Pro Tips
1. **Never Bluff Every Turn**: If you lie on every turn, experienced players will quickly challenge you and saddle you with massive card piles. Mix honest plays with clever bluffs.
2. **Track the 4 Suits**: There are only four of each rank in a 52-card deck. If you hold two Kings in your hand, and an opponent claims to play three Kings, you know there are only two remaining in the deck — a mathematically guaranteed bluff!
3. **Dump Trouble Cards Late in Cycles**: When the center pile has only 1 or 2 cards, calling a bluff or taking a risk has low penalty. When the pile has 20+ cards, challenges become high-stakes life-or-death moves!
4. **Use Voice Chat**: Read your opponents' vocal hesitation and banter in real time using the built-in WebRTC voice chat!
